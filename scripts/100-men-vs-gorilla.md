## Notes and fairness

- Silverback weight: wild adult males are roughly 300–440 lb (about 140–200 kg); "up to four hundred pounds" stays
  in that range.
- Strength: claims of "10x a human" are not proven; the script says "several times stronger".
- Canines: adult male gorillas have long canines, roughly 2 inches.
- The men's side is stated fairly: the script says the men only win if they all keep fighting. The verdict rests on
  morale (untrained, unarmed people stop charging after seeing others get hurt). That's our reasoning, not a lab fact.
- Original drawings only (the owner's sprite sheets). No real gorilla footage. SFX from `sfx-library/`.
