# 100 Men vs 1 Gorilla: who would win?

**Status: DRAFT** (only the owner marks APPROVED)

- Video #1 of the new Ink Brawl stick man format.
- Why this one first: we have no followers yet, so the stick men can't be "you" yet. We open with the internet's
  most controversial fight debate instead, and invite people to comment to be in the next brawl.
- Shape: 9:16 Short, about 55–60 s (also a 16:9 cut).
- ~165 spoken words.

## Script (spoken)

**Hook (0–3 s)**
One hundred men. One gorilla. Who would win?

**Why this video (3–10 s)**
This is Ink Brawl, and this is our very first video. We don't have followers yet, so instead of you, we're starting
with the most controversial debate on the internet. Soon, the stick men will be YOU.

**The fight**
Meet the gorilla. A silverback can weigh around four hundred pounds, and he's several times stronger than any man
here. Two-inch fangs. One swing sends men flying.

But the men have numbers. Only a few can reach him at once, so they come in waves.

Wave one: gone. Wave two: gone. The gorilla is winning.

**Turning point**
But a gorilla is built for short bursts, not a long fight. After a few minutes, he slows down. Twenty men grab his
arms. Twenty more grab his legs. And he goes down.

**Verdict + call to action**
Our verdict: the hundred men win. But it costs them. Dozens get knocked out first.

Do you agree? Comment who YOU think wins, and comment "ME" to be a stick man in the next brawl.

## Notes and fairness

- Silverback weight: wild adult males are roughly 300–440 lb (about 140–200 kg). "Around four hundred pounds" is the
  top end, so the script says "can weigh".
- Strength: claims of "10× a human" are not proven. Estimates vary, so the script says "several times stronger".
- Canines: adult male gorillas have long canines, roughly 2 inches.
- Stamina: gorillas fight in short, explosive bursts (charges and displays), not long sustained fights. The turning
  point rests on this, and it's presented as our reasoning, not a lab fact.
- Both sides get real wins on screen: the gorilla wins the first waves, and the men win through numbers. That is the
  usual reasoning on both sides of the 2025 viral debate.
